**The centerpiece of this version** - A fully interactive timeline showcasing **40+ major Bitcoin milestones** across 15+ years:

- **🏦 Banking Integration** - Major banks offering Bitcoin services (JPMorgan, PayPal, Fidelity)

- **🌍 Adoption & Integration** - Lightning Network growth, circular economies, merchant adoption
- **⚡ Technical Milestones** - Halvings, Taproot upgrade, protocol improvements

#### Recent Game-Changers (2024-2025)

- ✅ **Bitcoin ETF Approval** (January 2024) - BlackRock, Fidelity, Grayscale approved by SEC
- ✅ **$20B+ in ETF Inflows** - Fastest ETF adoption in history
- ✅ **Fourth Bitcoin Halving** (April 2024) - Supply reduced to 3.125 BTC per block
- ✅ **New All-Time High** - $73,000+ reached before halving (unprecedented)
- ✅ **MicroStrategy's 331,200 BTC** - Largest corporate Bitcoin holder
- ✅ **Trump Bitcoin Support** - Major political shift in 2024 campaign
- ✅ **US Bitcoin Reserve Bill** - Proposal for 1M BTC strategic reserve

### 🎯 Interactive Features

- **Category Filtering** - Filter milestones by type (political, banking, investment, etc.)
- **Expandable Cards** - Click any milestone to read full details
- **Impact Indicators** - Visual markers for high/medium/low impact events
- **Timeline Visualization** - Year-by-year journey with connecting animations
- **Statistics Dashboard** - Real-time counts of milestones, impacts, and categories
- **Color-Coded System** - Easy visual identification of event types
